Embedded Systems & Robotics

RAVEN 1.0

Year 2025
Category Robotics
Duration 1 Month
Achievement ROBOFEST Finalist

Project Overview

RAVEN 1.0 is an ESP32-based autonomous micromouse robot developed for accurate sensing and intelligent navigation in maze environments. The system uses three Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensors to perform real-time distance measurement, obstacle detection, and wall following.

The ESP32 acts as the core controller, enabling efficient sensor data processing and reliable real-time decision-making. This project provided strong hands-on experience in embedded systems, robotics, and hardware-software integration.

Achievement

ROBOFEST 2025 Finals: RAVEN 1.0 successfully qualified for the Finals of ROBOFEST 2025, organized by the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), showcasing its competitive performance and technical robustness.

Future Scope

RAVEN 1.0 serves as a foundation for future enhancements such as optimized navigation algorithms and smarter autonomous behaviors.
