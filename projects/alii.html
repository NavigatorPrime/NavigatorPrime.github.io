Digital Logic & Simulation

The ALII Project — Logic in Motion

Year 2024
Category Digital Logic Design
Duration 3 Months
Team Team Project

Project Overview

The ALII project demonstrates our rigorous problem-solving process in digital electronics. While the physical product showcases our craftsmanship, the virtual simulations represent the "brain" of the operation. This project highlighted the vital importance of simulation-driven design, bridging the gap between virtual waveforms and physical hardware output.

Technical Details

  • Precision Timing: Integrated a 555 Timer circuit to generate a stable clock pulse for synchronous operation.
  • Logic Validation: Used D-Flip Flops and Multiplexers to manage state transitions and ensure clean data flow to the 7-segment displays.
  • Error Mitigation: Pre-testing the gate logic in a virtual environment eliminated race conditions before moving to the breadboard.
