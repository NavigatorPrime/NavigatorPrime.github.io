Industrial Control System

Alga-Bot — Intelligent Photobioreactor

Year 2024
Category Control Systems & Signal Conditioning
Duration 4 Months
Team 3 Members

Project Overview

The Alga-Bot is a fully automated photobioreactor designed from the ground up as an end-to-end industrial control system. It monitors and regulates the environmental conditions necessary for optimal microalgae cultivation — light intensity, temperature, pH, and nutrient flow — through a closed-loop feedback architecture.

This project demonstrates real-world control engineering principles applied to a biological system: signal conditioning, DAQ integration, PID-based regulation, and multi-variable process control.

Technical Details

  • Custom signal conditioning circuit using LM358 op-amps for LDR voltage dividers, achieving precise light-intensity measurement with noise-filtered analog output
  • LabVIEW-based Data Acquisition (DAQ) system for real-time environmental monitoring — sampling at 10 Hz across 4 analog channels simultaneously
  • Dual-spectrum LED control loops (red 630nm + blue 460nm) with independent PWM regulation for photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) optimization
  • Closed-loop temperature control using NTC thermistor feedback with hysteresis deadband to prevent relay chatter
  • pH monitoring subsystem with buffered electrode interface and software-based calibration routine
  • Custom PCB layout for the analog front-end, designed in KiCad with proper ground plane partitioning

Challenges & Solutions

The primary challenge was achieving stable analog readings in an electrically noisy environment. The aquarium pumps and LED drivers introduced significant EMI. This was resolved through careful analog front-end design — shielded cables, decoupling capacitors at every op-amp supply pin, and a dedicated analog ground plane separated from the digital ground.

Another challenge was the non-linear response of the LDR sensors. A lookup table with linear interpolation was implemented in the LabVIEW VI to linearize the sensor output across the operating range.
